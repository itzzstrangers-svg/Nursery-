PLANT CARE

Simple Care Guide

💧

Watering

Check the soil before watering. Avoid keeping most plants continuously waterlogged. Requirements vary by plant and weather.

☀️

Sunlight

Some plants need direct sunlight while others prefer indirect light or shade. Always check the plant's requirements.

🌱

Soil

Use suitable, well-draining soil and containers with drainage holes to reduce excess moisture.

🌿

Fertilizer

Use fertilizer according to the plant and product instructions. Excess fertilizer can damage plants.

✂️

Pruning

Remove dead or damaged parts with clean tools. Avoid unnecessary pruning of healthy growth.

🪴

Repotting

Consider repotting when roots are crowded or the plant has outgrown its container.

SEASONAL GUIDE

Plants & Tips by Season

☀️

Summer

Monitor soil moisture and protect sensitive plants from excessive afternoon heat.

Examples:

Aloe Vera, Cactus, Hibiscus

🌧️

Monsoon

Focus on drainage, avoid unnecessary watering and watch for fungal problems.

Examples:

Tulsi, Ferns, Ixora

❄️

Winter

Many plants need less frequent watering during cooler periods.

Examples:

Marigold, Rose, Spinach
